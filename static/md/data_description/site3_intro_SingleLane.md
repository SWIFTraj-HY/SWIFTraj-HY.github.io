The site corresponding to the **Single-Lane Dataset** is located on the Nanjing Inner Ring West Line, an elevated segment of Nanjing’s urban expressway network. The observed section is a two-way, two-lane roadway with one lane per direction, where lane-changing maneuvers are not possible. Traffic videos were collected by two UAVs operating simultaneously. Vehicle trajectories were independently extracted from the videos recorded by each UAV and were subsequently stitched in post-processing to generate continuous vehicle trajectories over the observed road segment.

The dataset includes two weekday afternoon peak-hour observation periods: 15:20–17:20 on November 1, 2022, and 16:00–16:46 on November 14, 2022. The November 1 data cover approximately 1,000 m and consist of six sessions, F1–F6, with F5 captured diagonally to extend the observable road range. The November 14 data cover approximately 700 m and consist of three sessions, AD, BE, and CF.
